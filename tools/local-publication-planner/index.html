How the cost calculation works

Cash expenses = monthly operating cost + audience growth budget. Labor = hours per week x value per hour x 52 / 12. Setup recovery = setup cost / recovery months. Cost target = cash + labor + setup recovery. Placements = cost target / net placement amount, rounded up. A zero net placement amount leaves the placement count unset.

The model treats your entered weekly workload as constant across a 52-week year and spreads it across 12 months. It omits taxes, financing, and expenses you have not entered. Use a placement amount after any direct fees or delivery costs.
