Record the package terms that affect your cost

Our suggested purchase checklist is short enough to fill in while reviewing the offer. Leave unanswered items open until the seller clarifies them.

  • The exact package name, total charge, and whether there are recurring or optional charges.
  • The scope of any setup or advertising service, including who funds advertising spend.
  • What information can be exported and what happens to access if the service ends.
  • The written refund conditions, request method, and the support contact.

Use confirmed charges in the worksheet. Keep your hoped-for audience size and sponsorship sales as assumptions until you have actual results.
