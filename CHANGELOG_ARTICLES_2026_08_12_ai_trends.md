## Distribution draft

### Which AI trends should software teams plan for in 2026?

Software teams should plan around five signals: improving agents that still miss structured tasks, a March 2026 snapshot in which the open-closed Arena gap had reopened, defects in public coding benchmarks, successful agent hijacking in a public red-teaming competition, and AI rules with dated transitions.

Use these release checks:

- require an objective completion test and target reliability for every agent task;
- compare model quality, latency, cost, and failure behavior on the task being shipped;
- rerun owned regression cases after model, prompt, tool, or retrieval changes;
- authenticate the agent or delegating user, and bind delegated authority to that identity;
- use scoped, short-lived credentials with documented issuance, rotation, and revocation;
- limit tools, data, network, secrets, and lifetime; require approval for high-impact actions; retain identity-bound, tamper-evident logs; and review authorization separately from model robustness;
- test attacker-controlled content only in an isolated non-production harness with synthetic data, no production secrets, disposable credentials and targets, and production writes and egress disabled; inspect the visible response and exact tool calls, then check side effects;
- retain model identity, provider or deployer role and place of establishment or location, deployment region, market-placement and putting-into-service dates and locations, output-use locations, GPAI transition cohort, applicable compliance date, and legal-source version;
- for Article 50, retain synthetic output types, applicable duties, exception basis, disclosure form and timing, clarity, accessibility, marking and detectability decisions, the applicable data-protection regime and processing decision, and validation evidence;
- for high-risk systems, retain the Article 6 rationale, Annex classification, Section B status, and applicable Chapter III date;
- also retain legacy status, the significant-design-change decision, and any public-authority deadline.
